Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I upload bank statements?

A: Open the Analyze tab in the app, tap Upload Statement, and pick your PDF or Excel statement file. The AI will automatically clean descriptions and parse transactions.

Q: How does Google Drive Sync work?

A: Navigate to Settings -> Cloud Backup and sign in with Google. Toggle Auto-Backup to keep an encrypted backup copy safely synced to your private Google Drive account.

Q: Is my financial data stored on your servers?

A: No. Expense Analyzer does not maintain central user database servers. All your transactions stay stored locally on your Android device in encrypted Room database files.

Q: How do I enable On-Device AI?

A: Go to Settings -> AI Agent and tap Download Model to download a lightweight offline LLM. Once downloaded, toggle Enable On-Device AI for 100% offline analysis.
