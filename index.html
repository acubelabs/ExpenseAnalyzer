Engineered For Privacy & Scale

Core Architecture Pillars

Built from the ground up for power users who demand full control over their financial records.

Physical Multi-Tenant Isolation

Separate SQLite database files (`expense_analyzer_user.db`) are created on disk for each signed-in profile. Profile A's database is physically closed and encrypted when Profile B logs in.

  • Zero cross-user data leakage on shared tablets
  • Isolated encryption keys and PIN locks

Multi-Account & Multi-Bank

Unify transactions across all financial institutions: HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, Credit Cards, and Cash Wallets.

  • Automated account balance reconciliation
  • Credit vs Debit split transaction support

Multi-Document Support

Direct parsing of digital PDF bank statements (password protected), Excel spreadsheets (`.xlsx`/`.xls`), and receipt photos enhanced via OpenCV and ML Kit OCR.

  • Scans first 100 rows for auto header alignment
  • 40+ supported date formats & timestamps
