About Expense Analyzer

Expense Analyzer is a native Android application designed to simplify personal money management. It helps users track daily expenses, manage budgets, scan receipt images, analyze financial trends, and back up financial records securely.

Key Features:

  • Expense & Income Tracking: Easily log and categorize your daily transactions.
  • Budget Management: Set monthly budgets and get alerts before overspending.
  • Receipt Scanning: Capture and process receipts directly on your device.
  • Finora AI Assistant: Ask financial insights using local or cloud AI models.
  • Secure Google Drive Backup: Keep your financial data safe with encrypted backups to your personal Google Drive.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Expense Analyzer ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Expense Analyzer stores all your expense logs, categories, and account balances locally on your Android device. We do not sell or share your personal financial data with any third parties.

2. Google User Data & Drive Integration

If you choose to use the Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup feature, Expense Analyzer accesses only the specific backup files created by the application (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file or app data folder). Your Google account information is used strictly to authenticate and store your encrypted backup file in your private Google Drive storage.

3. Data Security

Your local data is secured on-device using Android's native security features and EncryptedSharedPreferences.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You can delete all your financial records at any time directly within the app settings or by uninstalling the application. You can also delete your Google Drive backup file directly from your Google account.
