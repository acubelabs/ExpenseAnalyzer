Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

At Expense Analyzer ("we", "our", or "us"), privacy is built into our core architecture. This Privacy Policy explains how personal and financial information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our native Android application.

1. Local-First Data Storage

Expense Analyzer operates primarily as a local-first application. All your expense records, accounts, custom classification rules, budgets, and uploaded statement files are stored locally on your device in an isolated Room Database.

2. Google Drive Backup & User Data Access

If you explicitly enable Google Cloud Sync, Expense Analyzer accesses your Google Drive account using the restricted scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file) to manage backup files created solely by this application.

  • Usage: Your Google account email and OAuth token are used exclusively to authenticate and transfer encrypted backup files to your private Google Drive storage.
  • Isolation: We do not access, view, or store any other files in your Google Drive account.
  • No Commercial Use: We do not sell, rent, share, or use your financial or Google user data for advertising or marketing.

3. AI Assistant & On-Device Processing

Expense Analyzer supports both Cloud AI (Gemini BYOK) and Offline On-Device AI (LiteRT-LM):

  • Cloud AI (Gemini BYOK): If you choose to enable Cloud AI, your queries are processed via your personal encrypted API key stored safely in Android KeyStore.
  • On-Device Local AI: If you download local models, all financial chat queries, receipt OCR corrections, and category classifications run 100% offline on your device without transmitting data over the internet.

4. Security Protocols

Your data is protected locally using Android KeyStore encryption and optional PIN / Biometric locks. Multi-account isolation ensures that switching accounts swaps local storage, preventing cross-user data exposure.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You retain full ownership of your data at all times. You can wipe your local records directly in the app settings, or delete your backup file from your Google Drive account at any time.
