Our Mission

Why We Built Expense Analyzer

Modern financial applications increasingly demand that users upload personal bank statements, card logs, and transaction details to central cloud servers. At Acube Labs, we believe financial data is too sensitive to be pooled remotely.

We engineered Expense Analyzer on a strict Local-First, Zero-Server Philosophy. Every bank statement is parsed directly on your Android device's CPU/GPU, every transaction is stored inside a physical local SQLite database, and all AI queries default to on-device MediaPipe LLM execution.
